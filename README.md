# ReponaHQ

**Independent software. Thoughtfully built.**

ReponaHQ is the home for independent software products focused on making complex workflows simpler, more transparent, and easier to manage.

We believe good software should be practical, reliable, and built with care.

## Our products

### [Repona](https://repona.dev)

**Developer tools with architectural governance at their core.**

Repona helps engineering teams understand their repositories, capture architectural decisions, and keep implementation aligned with intent.

Built around a simple principle: **AI can propose. Humans decide.**

[Explore Repona](https://repona.dev)

## Our approach

- **Thoughtful engineering** — Build for maintainability, not just delivery.
- **Human ownership** — Automation should support decisions, not silently make them.
- **Practical simplicity** — Solve real problems without unnecessary complexity.
- **Transparency by design** — Make decisions, changes, and their reasoning visible.
